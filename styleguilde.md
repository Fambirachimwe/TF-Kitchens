1. Design Direction
Design Theme
The website design direction should follow a:
•	Modern luxury aesthetic
•	Interior design inspired layout
•	Spacious and elegant visual hierarchy
•	Editorial-style presentation
•	Premium construction/interior feel
•	Minimalistic but visually rich experience
The inspiration design uses:
•	Large immersive imagery
•	Soft rounded corners
•	Generous white space
•	Editorial typography
•	Neutral luxurious backgrounds
•	Card-based content sections
•	Clean navigation
•	Grid-driven layouts
•	Sophisticated visual balance
TF Kitchen Design should adapt this style while incorporating the company’s brand colors.
